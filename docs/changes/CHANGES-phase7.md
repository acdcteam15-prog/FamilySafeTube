# Phase 7 — Quiz extras

## 1. Sound-only questions ("hear the animal, pick the picture")
- Some questions play ONLY the animal's sound; the name shows as "❓" and is hidden until the child answers.
  After a correct tap the name is revealed and spoken, followed by the sound again.
- Only cards that have a sound (built-in or your recording) are asked this way; about 3 in 10 questions for those cards.
- A wrong tap repeats only what was asked (the sound), not the answer.
- Switch off in Settings → "Include sound-only questions".

## 2. Progress view (Dashboard → "🧩 Quiz progress")
- Total answers and overall accuracy; **words learned**, **words that need practice**, and how many are not tried yet,
  named in the quiz language.
- Learned = at least 3 right answers and 75 %+ correct. Needs practice = 3+ attempts and under 50 % correct.
  Forgiving on purpose: a baby taps by chance, and a single early miss never marks a word as a problem.
- Only cards switched on in the editor are counted. Words that need practice also come back more often in the quiz (already the case).

## 3. End-of-round screen
- After 5 correct answers: big stars, "Great job!", the phone says "Well done!" in the quiz language,
  then two large buttons: 🔁 Again or 👋 Bye-bye (says goodbye, then closes the quiz).
- No timer, no pressure, nothing extra is earned. Wrong answers never reset the stars.
- The usual screen-time limits still apply: when time is up the quiz closes as before.

## Tests: 150 (all pass here)
New: question modes in the engine (rate, only cards with sounds), audio plans for each mode,
progress levels and ordering, round tracker.

## Not verified here
The Compose screens and animations, speech output, and the real feel of the sound-only questions (needs a baby and a phone).

- [ ] After 5 right answers: end-of-round screen with Again / Bye-bye; Again continues, Bye-bye speaks and closes
- [ ] Dashboard shows quiz progress after a few rounds (learned / needs practice / not tried)
